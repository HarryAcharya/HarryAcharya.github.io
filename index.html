Student Loan Repayment Risk: A Target-Leakage Post-Mortem

My first model scored a perfect 1.0 ROC-AUC. I treated that as a symptom, not a success: I had leaked the target into my own feature set. I rebuilt the pipeline with regex-enforced leakage guards and shipped an honest 0.946 ROC-AUC on 4,756 institutions, powering a three-tier triage that captures 73% of high-risk institutions in the top 23% of the book (a 3.2× lift). Then my headline finding, that for-profit status drives risk, failed multivariate control, so I retracted my own recommendation. The full post-mortem is published as a merged pull request and model card.
